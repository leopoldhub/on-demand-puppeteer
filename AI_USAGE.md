# AI Usage Disclaimer

This project was conceived, directed, and accepted by a human. Generative AI was heavily involved in its realization, including implementation, documentation, review support, and test creation.

The use of AI does not replace human ownership of the project. Architectural decisions, intended behavior, security constraints, and final changes remain under human control and review.

## Risk Assessment

The additional risk associated with AI-assisted development is considered low for this project because:

- The project has a small and deliberately constrained scope.
- Its responsibilities and trust boundaries are explicitly documented.
- The implementation is reviewable and covered by unit, integration, and end-to-end tests.
- AI-generated changes are subject to human direction, repository constraints, and verification before acceptance.

This assessment is not a guarantee that the software is defect-free or suitable for every environment. CDP provides full browser control, and persistent browser profiles can contain sensitive authenticated data. Operators remain responsible for reviewing the software, protecting access, and validating it for their deployment and threat model.
